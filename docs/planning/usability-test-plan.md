# Usability test plan

Closes the gap in [[self-assessment]]: "no usability test with real users". Three short sessions, five tasks each, notes, and one change made because of them. Results go in [[usability-results]] (create after the first session). Related: [[human-todo]] (section G), [[design-system]].

## Setup
- 3 people who haven't seen the app (classmates, family). Build installed on their phone or yours.
- 10 minutes each. Say: "I'm testing the app, not you. Think aloud." Don't help unless stuck 30 seconds.

## Tasks (success = done unaided)
1. Create a trip with dates and a budget.
2. Add an activity with a start time to day 2.
3. Log an expense that's split unevenly.
4. Find out who owes whom and mark a payment as paid.
5. Add three checklist items from a pasted list.

## What to write down per task
Done / needed a hint / failed; where they hesitated; their words; any accessibility issue (text size, VoiceOver/TalkBack).

## After the sessions
- Pick the most common stumble and fix it; note the before and after in [[decisions]].
- Add one line to the usability section of [[self-assessment]] and re-score UX.
