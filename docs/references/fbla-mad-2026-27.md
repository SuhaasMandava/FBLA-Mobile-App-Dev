# FBLA Mobile Application Development 2026-27: key facts

Summary of the official guidelines (PDF "Updated August 2026", FBLA copyright; don't paste it into notes or a public repo). Topic: **Together We Go: Group Trip Planner**. High school, teams of 1 to 3, presentation event.

## Format
- Two rounds: preliminary, then final (top scorers per section advance: 2 sections → top 6, 3 → top 4, 4 or 5 → top 3, more than 5 → top 2).
- Each round: **3 min set-up, 7 min presentation (1-min warning), 3 min Q&A.** Times are exclusive; no shifting time, no talking to judges during set-up.
- Preliminary: ~10' x 10' space, a table and judges' chairs, internet provided but may be unreliable (bring a backup), **no power**, no projector. Final: projector, screen, power and table provided if in a conference room; bring your own HDMI adapters.
- Max **3 personal devices**. No external speakers (audio must come from the presenting device). Clickers allowed.
- Judges may **not scan or click links/QR codes**; they may be shown. No food (display only) or animals. Leave nothing with judges. No unauthorized recording.
- Every team member must take part in presenting.
- Penalties: 5 points for dress code violation, 5 for arriving late. Code of Conduct or Honor Code violations can disqualify.
- Eligibility basics: dues paid by 11:59 pm ET March 1; registered for NLC and in the official hotel block; photo ID at check-in; team from one chapter; top-10 NLC finishers in this event can't repeat it.

## What the presentation must cover
- Platform used (Android, iOS or Windows Phone allowed); must run standalone with no programming errors; must be smartphone deployable; no app store needed.
- Demonstrate usability and functionality to the judges.
- Show, where applicable: README, source code, documentation of templates/libraries used, copyright and sources of materials.
- Design and code quality (explain design and implementation), UX (map the user journey, explain design rationale, ease of use and accessibility), and **social media integration**.
- Information must come from credible, documented sources; copyrighted material, images, logos and trademarks must be documented.
